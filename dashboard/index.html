Clinical Interpretation & Trade-off Analysis

In standard 1-hour immediate alerting (Baseline), isolated transient sensor spikes or brief physiological instability generate over 2,100 false alert episodes across non-septic ICU stays, triggering severe alert fatigue. Experiment 5 introduces a 2-out-of-3 hour sustained confirmation policy that eliminates 57.5% of all false alarm episodes and reduces non-septic patient alert rate by 42.2%, while preserving a 12.0-hour median clinical lead time prior to sepsis onset for detected patients.
